## GENERATION SETTINGS FOR INSTAGRAM

**Video Specifications:**
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Quality:** 1080px minimum (recommended: 1440x2560)
- **Format:** MP4 or MOV
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Styling:** Cinematic, Premium, Professional

**Instagram-Specific Optimizations:**
- First 2 seconds must hook viewers (stop thumb scrolling)
- Captions/text should be readable in 1 second
- Quick cuts every 1-2 seconds keep engagement high
- Bottom 20% of screen left for Instagram UI/buttons
- Vertical black bars acceptable, but fill frame when possible
