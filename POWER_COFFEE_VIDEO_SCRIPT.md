## Cinematic Direction Notes

### Tone & Style
- **Premium, aspirational, authentic**
- Real people, real moments, real energy
- Warm, golden lighting in early morning scenes
- Clean, modern aesthetic for workplace scenes
- Dynamic camera movements (slight zoom-ins, smooth pans)

### Color Palette
- Golden hour oranges and warm tones (morning scenes)
- Blues and grays (professional/office scenes)
- Vibrant greens (outdoor/fitness scenes)
- Product packaging colors: navy, gold, white (brand consistency)

### Audio Strategy
- Upbeat instrumental: 100-120 BPM
- Clear, confident voiceover (male or female, warm tone)
- Subtle sound effects: coffee pouring, sipping, gym sounds
- Music builds throughout, peaks at impact section

### People & Diversity
**Required:** 8-10 diverse individuals
- Age range: 25-55
- Gender: Mix of men and women
- Ethnicities: Diverse representation
- Professions: Athletes, entrepreneurs, professionals, parents, creatives
- All should appear genuinely energized and authentic

### Product Placement Strategy
1. **Hero Shot** (Scene 2): Close-up of packaging, clear brand visibility
2. **In-Hand Moments** (Scenes 2 & 3): People actively using the product
3. **Social Proof** (Scene 4): Product visible in testimonial scenes
4. **Final Showcase** (Scene 5): Premium product presentation with CTA

## Conversion Strategy

### Why This Works:
1. **Emotional Connection**: Shows real people achieving their goals
2. **Product Benefit**: Clearly demonstrates what Power Coffee enables
3. **Social Proof**: Testimonials build trust and credibility
4. **Action-Oriented**: People are *doing* things, not just talking about coffee
5. **Authentic Diversity**: Represents the actual customer base
6. **Clear CTA**: Amazon link makes purchase friction minimal
7. **Brand Alignment**: Every scene reinforces "fuel the people who show up fully"

### Expected Metrics:
- **CTR Goal**: 3-5% click-through to Amazon
- **View Completion**: 60%+ watch full video
- **Conversion**: 5-8% of clicks convert to purchases
- **Ideal Platforms**: YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn
