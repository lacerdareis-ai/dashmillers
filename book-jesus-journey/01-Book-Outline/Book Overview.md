# Book Overview

**Working Title:** *Called Across the Water — Our Journey With Jesus to America*

**Back to:** [[Home]]

---

## The Book in One Sentence

A family memoir about how faith in Jesus gave us the courage to leave everything we knew, move to the United States, and discover that He was already there waiting for us.

---

## Vision

This book is for:
- Immigrant families navigating faith in a new country
- Anyone in a season of major life transition
- People who feel called by God but are afraid of the cost
- Churches wanting to understand the immigrant experience through a gospel lens

---

## Core Message

> **God's faithfulness does not have a passport.**
> He is Lord of every nation, every culture, every transition.

---

## Tone & Voice

- Personal, warm, honest — we do not hide the hard parts
- Rooted in Scripture without being preachy
- Bilingual flavor — moments in our original language woven in
- Hopeful, not triumphalistic

---

## Comparable Books

- *The Hiding Place* — Corrie ten Boom (faith under pressure)
- *Surprised by Oxford* — Carolyn Weber (faith meets new world)
- *The God Who Sees* — Karen Gonzalez (immigrant faith stories)
