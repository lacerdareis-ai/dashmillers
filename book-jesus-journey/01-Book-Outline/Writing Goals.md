## Weekly Writing Practice

- **Goal:** Write at least 3 times per week
- **Session length:** 30–60 minutes minimum
- **Method:** Story capture first, polish later

## Guiding Principles for Writing

1. **Truth first** — tell it like it actually was, not how it sounds better
2. **Protect people** — change names if needed, ask permission
3. **Scripture as foundation** — let the Word frame every major moment
4. **Rest is part of the work** — prayer and silence before every session
