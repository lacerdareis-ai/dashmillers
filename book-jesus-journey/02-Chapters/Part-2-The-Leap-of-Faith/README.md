# Part 2 — The Leap of Faith

**Back to:** [[Home]] | [[Structure and Chapters]]

> *"By faith Abraham obeyed when he was called to go out to a place that he was to receive as an inheritance. And he went out, not knowing where he was going."* — Hebrews 11:8

---

## Purpose of This Part

This is the turning point. The yes. The goodbye. The crossing. Readers should feel the weight of what it means to step out in faith when the outcome is uncertain. Abraham is the model.
