## Core Themes

These threads run through the whole book. Every chapter should connect to at least one.

### Faith
[[Faith]] — What it means to trust God with what you cannot see

### Identity
[[Identity]] — Who we are in Christ across cultures and borders

### Calling
[[Calling]] — Being summoned by God to something specific and costly

### Belonging
[[Belonging]] — The hunger for home, and finding it in Jesus and His people

### Obedience
[[Obedience]] — The cost and the joy of doing what God says

### Community
[[Community]] — The Body of Christ as family in a foreign land

### God's Provision
[[God's Provision]] — How God meets practical and spiritual needs

### Lament
[[Lament]] — Honest grief as a spiritual practice

### Transformation
[[Transformation]] — Who God makes us through the hard road

### Hope
[[Hope]] — The unshakeable expectation rooted in who God is
