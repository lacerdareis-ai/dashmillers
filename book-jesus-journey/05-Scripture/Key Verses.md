## New Testament Passages on Belonging & Foreignness

- Hebrews 11:13-16 — We are strangers and pilgrims
- Ephesians 2:19 — No longer foreigners but fellow citizens
- 1 Peter 2:11 — Dear friends, as foreigners and exiles...
- Philippians 3:20 — Our citizenship is in heaven

---

## Old Testament Narratives That Mirror Our Journey

- Abraham — called from Ur to an unknown land (Genesis 12)
- Joseph — carried to Egypt against his will, God's plan revealed (Genesis 37-50)
- Ruth — left her homeland out of love and loyalty (Ruth 1)
- Daniel — faithful in Babylon (Daniel 1-6)
- Esther — positioned in a foreign land for such a time as this (Esther 4:14)
