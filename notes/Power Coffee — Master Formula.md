## 🆕 Changelog — Sodium Bicarbonate Addition

> [!note] What changed
> Added **0.3% sodium bicarbonate** (22.5 g batch / 45 mg per serving).
> Pulled 0.3% from Instant Coffee (42.3% → 42.0%) to keep total at 7,500 g.

**Why this change:**
- Reduces coffee acidity / bitterness → smoother cup
- Buffers pH across shelf life → better stability with protein + spices
- Synergy with Gum Arabic (hydrates cleaner at neutral pH)
- Enables a "gentle on stomach" narrative

**Impact on Nutrition Facts:**
- Adds ~12 mg sodium per serving (rounds to 15 mg on label)
- Still <1% DV → **"low sodium" claim is preserved**
- No change to calories, protein, carbs, or fat

## ⚠️ Production Notes

> [!warning] Blending Sequence
> Add **sodium bicarbonate late** in the dry-mix sequence. Distribute evenly — avoid clumping near moisture-sensitive ingredients (esp. matcha).

> [!danger] Do NOT combine with acidic ingredients
> Sodium bicarbonate + citric acid / ascorbic acid / citrus flavoring → CO₂ gas reaction.
> Current formula has **no acidic leaveners** → safe as-is.
> Re-evaluate if flavor variants (lemon, berry, etc.) are developed.
